# Two-week practitioner pilot

**Status: planned; no adoption or time-savings claims have been made.** Recruit 5–10 HR practitioners who did not build the system. Use synthetic personas and standardized cases only.

## Protocol

1. Consent participants and explain that this evaluates a workflow, not their performance.
2. Baseline 12 balanced cases completed manually; capture time, answer quality, confidence, and escalation.
3. Train participants with a 20-minute scenario and control-point walkthrough.
4. Complete the same difficulty mix with the system; counterbalance case order.
5. Record completion time, rubric score, correct escalation, citations checked, overrides and reason, confidence, friction, and system errors.
6. Interview participants using the same questions; code themes without names.
7. Fix no more than three prioritized failure patterns, rerun the affected tests, and publish a de-identified report.

## Metrics, criteria, and decisions

These are three different things.

- **Metrics describe what the pilot observed.** Report median and distribution, not only averages, for completion time, rubric score, escalation, citations checked, and overrides. Label estimated time saved separately from observed time saved. Do not imply statistical significance from this sample.
- **Acceptance criteria define what must be true before the next stage.** They are the [acceptance criteria](evaluation-methodology.md#acceptance-criteria-for-redesigned-routing) set before testing, including no critical-control failures. A pilot of this size cannot show that missed escalations are rare; it can show whether they occur.
- **Risk owners decide what residual risk is acceptable.** Employee Relations, Legal, and the People Operations owner decide whether to proceed. No percentage makes that decision for them.
